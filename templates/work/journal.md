# Journal

Appended to, never rewritten. Three lines are a legitimate entry, and that cheapness is
the point: this is the only file that survives compaction, because compaction gives no
turn in which to write anything.

Reasons, not chronology. The transcript already holds the order of events, and that is
the part worth least.

Append when something is learned, and always when an approach was rejected, when a
measurement produced a number, when a trap was found, or when a skill or document being
filled in along the way gained a point. The last one goes here rather than into the
document itself: editing a live document mid-task is expensive, and handover is where
the journal is edited into it in one focused step.
